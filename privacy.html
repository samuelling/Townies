TOWNIES

(Water) Townies: a city game set in the real Watertown, Massachusetts.

Privacy

Townies doesn’t collect, store or share any information about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Your saved cities stay on your device, in the app’s own storage. The game runs from files inside the app and doesn’t send what you do anywhere. The one exception is the option to refresh the map from live OpenStreetMap data: that asks OpenStreetMap’s public servers for the map of Watertown, and the request contains nothing but the map area. The map and town data it shows are public records and open data, listed under Help → Behind the scenes.

If this ever changes, this page will say so first.

Last updated October 2026.
